Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 6, 2026 | App Name: RoyelFood

1. Introduction

Welcome to RoyelFood ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (RoyelFood) and related services.

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that helps us deliver and improve our food ordering and delivery services:

  • Personal Identification Information: Name, phone number (collected during Firebase Phone Authentication / OTP verification), and delivery addresses.
  • Order & Transaction Data: Information regarding the food items you purchase, order history, special instructions, and transaction reference records.
  • Device & Log Data: Automatically collected technical information such as your mobile device name, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and application error logs via third-party services like Firebase Crashlytics for performance and stability monitoring.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for various business and operational purposes, including:

  • To create, manage, and authenticate your user account.
  • To process, fulfill, and deliver your food orders efficiently.
  • To communicate with you regarding order updates, delivery status, customer support, and administrative notices.
  • To monitor app performance, fix technical bugs, and secure against fraudulent activities through Firebase infrastructure.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. However, we utilize trusted third-party cloud and infrastructure services that process data on our behalf to operate the app:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore Database, Cloud Functions, and Crashlytics): Used for secure user logins, storing order data, backend execution, and monitoring application crashes.
  • Payment Gateways: (e.g., PhonePe SDK) If applicable, to securely process digital transactions. These services handle your financial details in accordance with their own strict privacy standards.

5. Data Security

We implement commercially acceptable security measures (such as encrypted data transmission over HTTPS and secure Firebase security rules) to protect your personal information. However, no method of electronic transmission or data storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

  • We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or to comply with applicable legal obligations.
  • Account Deletion: You have the right to request the deletion of your account and associated data. You can request data deletion by contacting us directly at support@dashtechsolution.com, and we will purge your profile and order data from our active databases.

7. Children's Privacy

Our application is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can take immediate removal actions.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any modifications.
